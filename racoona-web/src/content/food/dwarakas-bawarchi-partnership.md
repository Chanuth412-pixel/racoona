## Strategic Partnership Overview

Racoona AI's Hospitality Division is actively partnering with **Dwaraka's Bawarchi**, a leading culinary landmark located in Ashburn, VA. 

By integrating predictive sales telemetry, targeted local customer acquisition channels, and streamlined digital ordering, Racoona AI enables culinary brands to capture higher margin direct consumer demand.

### Core Objectives

1. **Digital Ordering Optimization:** Replacing fragmented third-party dispatch fees with automated direct online channels.
2. **Predictive Inventory & Demand:** Utilizing local dining analytics to predict peak kitchen throughput.
3. **Omnichannel Brand Reach:** Expanding brand visibility and customer loyalty across Northern Virginia.
